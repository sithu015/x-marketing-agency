Case Study: Landmark United Holdings
ClientLandmark United
DivisionsSpirits & Fashion
StrategyBrand Prestige
ChannelsMeta & TikTok

Challenge & Strategic Blueprint

Landmark United Holdings needed to establish distinct, commanding market presence across Myanmar for both its premium imported spirits (Löwen Samt, Ron Millonario 15) and its retail apparel lines. X Marketing Agency architected a dual-funnel strategy: combining high-end editorial visual design with short-form vertical videos on TikTok and Facebook Reels to build brand prestige and drive retail customer footfall.

Creative Direction & Visual Pillars

Liquor & Spirits

Warm amber ambient lighting, luxury bottle typography, editorial framing, and historical heritage storytelling capturing European prestige and collector appeal.

Fashion & Apparel

Minimalist cloud-textured street photography, clean typography, seasonal lookbooks, and high-energy 9:16 vertical transition reels for youthful engagement.

Production Craft & Art Direction

  • Cinematography & Studio Lighting: Dedicated on-location and studio production with specialized lighting rigs and gimbal-stabilized 4K capture.
  • Editorial Color Grading & Sound: Film-grade color grading, product retouching, and custom sound design tailored for short-form engagement.
  • Compliance & Brand Integrity: Proactive creative vetting against Meta and TikTok advertising policies and regional community guidelines.
