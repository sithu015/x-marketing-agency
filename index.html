Case Study: Landmark United Holdings — Dual-Division Brand Acceleration
Client: Landmark United
Divisions: Spirits & Fashion
Monthly Assets: 46 Deliverables
Core Channels: FB & TikTok

1. Challenge & Strategic Growth Blueprint

Landmark United Holdings needed to establish distinct, commanding market presence across Myanmar for both its premium imported spirits (Löwen Samt, Ron Millonario 15) and its retail apparel lines. X Marketing Agency architected a dual-funnel strategy: combining high-end editorial visual design with short-form vertical videos on TikTok and Facebook Reels to build brand prestige and drive retail customer footfall.

2. Content Architecture & Monthly Deliverables

Division Facebook Visual Posts TikTok HD Videos Monthly Total
Liquor & Spirits 16 Visual Photo Posts 7 Vertical Short Videos 23 Deliverables
Fashion & Apparel 16 Visual Photo Posts 7 Vertical Short Videos 23 Deliverables
TOTAL OUTPUT 32 Visual Posts / Mo 14 Short Videos / Mo 46 High-Res Assets / Mo

*Vertical short videos are cross-repurposed across TikTok and Facebook Reels to maximize cross-platform reach.

3. Production Quality Standards

  • Dedicated Shoot Days: 2–3 on-site production days each month with dedicated lighting and videography crews.
  • Refinement Policy: Up to 2 refinement rounds per asset within the approved creative brief to ensure exact brand alignment.
  • Compliance Assurance: Proactive vetting against Meta and TikTok alcohol advertising regulations and local platform standards.
  • Full Brand Ownership: Full commercial marketing rights for all approved visuals and produced videos.
