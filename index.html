Case Study: Landmark United Holdings — Dual-Division Brand Acceleration
Client: Landmark United
Divisions: Spirits & Fashion
Strategy: Brand Prestige
Channels: Meta & TikTok

1. Challenge & Strategic Growth Blueprint

Landmark United Holdings needed to establish distinct, commanding market presence across Myanmar for both its premium imported spirits (Löwen Samt, Ron Millonario 15) and its retail apparel lines. X Marketing Agency architected a dual-funnel strategy: combining high-end editorial visual design with short-form vertical videos on TikTok and Facebook Reels to build brand prestige and drive retail customer footfall.

2. Creative Direction & Visual Pillars

Liquor & Spirits (Löwen Samt & Ron Millonario 15)

Warm amber ambient lighting, luxury bottle typography, editorial framing, and historical heritage storytelling that captures European prestige and collector appeal.

Fashion & Apparel (Consuo & Monic)

Minimalist cloud-textured street and lifestyle photography, clean typography, seasonal lookbooks, and high-energy 9:16 vertical transition reels for youthful engagement.

3. Production Craft & Art Direction

  • Cinematography & Studio Lighting: Dedicated on-location and studio production crews with specialized lighting rigs and gimbal-stabilized 4K capture.
  • Editorial Color Grading & Sound: Bespoke film-grade color grading, high-end product retouching, and custom sound design tailored for short-form engagement.
  • Compliance & Brand Integrity: Proactive creative vetting against Meta and TikTok advertising policies and regional community guidelines.
